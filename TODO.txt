Project Architecture:

│
├── World

│
├── Math Library

├── Collision System

│    │    └── Detect possible colliding pairs
│    ├── Narrowphase                                             ###
│    │    ├── SAT / GJK / EPA / Circle tests                     // [SAT, CircleCircle, CirclePolygon Brute Force]
│    │    └── Generates ContactManifold

│         ├── Restitution, friction, impulses

├── Integrator
│    ├── Force accumulation
│    ├── Velocity + position update
│    └── Damping / sleep logic
│
├── Constraint Solver
│    ├── Joints (hinge, spring, slider)
│    ├── Motors / limits
│    └── Iterative solvers (Gauss-Seidel, PGS)

├── Scene Interface (API)
│    ├── AddBody(), RemoveBody()
